Mobility Digital Twin for FIFA World Cup 2026 in Atlanta

Downtown Atlanta faces unprecedented pedestrian and vehicular convergence during eight FIFA World Cup 2026 matches. Shadowed police officers and stadium parking operations to frame the operational bottleneck, architected the data and simulation framework, and delivered a real-time digital twin cutting turnaround from days to minutes.

  • Reconciled competing operational priorities between stadium security, police patrol, parking operators, and GDOT traffic controllers.
  • Enabled live scenario simulation of street closures and crowd diversions without slowing decision speed.
  • Featured prominently by the Georgia Tech College of Engineering and regional transit leadership.
