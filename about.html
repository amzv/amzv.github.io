01 / International Council of the Aeronautical Sciences (ICAS)

Liaison for Mexico // September 2024 – Present

Recognizing an absence of formal Mexican representation within the premier global aeronautical council, I brokered strategic relationships connecting the Mexican Federation of the Aerospace Industry (FEMIA), the Mexican Council of Aerospace Education (COMEA), and top engineering universities directly with ICAS executive leadership. This established Mexico's inaugural national delegation, translating international council value into targeted domestic manufacturing and research priorities.

Simultaneously, initiated bilateral academic exchange agreements between Georgia Tech and two prominent Mexican institutions. This pipeline has already generated admissions for doctoral candidates and facilitated cross-border exchange programs involving more than 20 students.

02 / Head of the Academy — Georgia Tech ASDL

Aerospace Systems Design Laboratory // August 2025 – Present

At Georgia Tech's Aerospace Systems Design Laboratory—one of the world's premier aerospace systems engineering institutes—I direct the internal Academy program. Responsibilities include designing and moderating weekly peer-support forums for 80+ graduate researchers, curating advanced technical debate topics, and synthesizing complex methodological questions for lab director Prof. Dimitri Mavris.

Under this leadership, the laboratory achieved a record number of internal research proposals and earned special commendation from the Vice Provost of Graduate Education at the Georgia Tech Graduate Education Expo. Additionally identified operational gaps in defense and proposal logistics, launching a cross-cohort coordination program that strengthened lab-wide collaboration.

03 / Mentorship & STEM Pipeline: F1 in Schools

Workshop Coordinator // Preparatoria Panamericana // 2017 – 2022

Early in my engineering career, founded and coordinated a multi-year competition incubator for high school students competing in the global F1 in Schools challenge. Developed an end-to-end curriculum integrating CAD surfacing, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), CNC manufacturing, telemetry analysis, and enterprise fundraising.

Recruited and managed co-instructors to scale delivery to over 100 students, shortlisting 20 high-potential engineers for targeted one-on-one mentorship. Teams under my direction qualified for two world finals and earned a world Top-15 global finish.
